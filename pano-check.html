The viewer's ray matrix negates its third column, so yaw = 0 centres u = 0.5: the render below must show band 6 on the crosshair, with 7 to its right and 5 to its left. Numbers running the other way means the sphere is mirrored; a different band in the centre means the yaw origin is wrong — which it was, by half a turn, until that negation was spotted.
